About WHprompts

Empowering creators worldwide with free, ready-to-use AI photo prompts.

Welcome to WHprompts — your trusted destination for high-quality Gemini AI photo prompts. We're a small, passionate team of creators, photographers, and AI enthusiasts who believe that beautiful AI-generated imagery should be accessible to everyone, not just tech experts.

Our Story

WHprompts was born in 2025 when we noticed a frustrating gap: AI image tools like Google Gemini, Media.io, and Midjourney were producing stunning results, but only for people who knew the "secret language" of effective prompting. Casual users were stuck with mediocre outputs because they didn't know how to write prompts with the right lighting, camera angles, poses, and details.

So we started writing them ourselves — testing, refining, and organizing hundreds of prompts until we had a curated collection that produced beautiful, consistent, magazine-quality results. Today, that collection has grown into 60+ prompts across 11 categories covering everything from cinematic couple photos and festival portraits to birthday celebrations and professional headshots.

Our Mission

To democratize AI creativity by providing the world's best free library of copy-paste photo prompts — helping millions of people turn their ideas into stunning visuals without technical barriers.
