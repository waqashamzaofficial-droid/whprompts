What Are Gemini AI Photo Prompts?

Gemini AI photo prompts are carefully written text instructions that tell an AI model exactly what to create. A good prompt includes the subject, pose, clothing, lighting, background, camera style, and mood. Google Gemini, one of the most advanced AI models, uses these instructions to generate or transform images that look natural, cinematic, and professional.

Our prompts are written specifically for Google Gemini and similar tools like Media.io's Image-to-Image workflow. They include photography terms like "85mm lens", "golden hour", "shallow depth of field", and "cinematic lighting" — the kind of details that dramatically improve output quality.
